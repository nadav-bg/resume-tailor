Operations Lead — Scale-up SaaS Company (Tel Aviv, Hybrid)

About the role:
We're a Series B SaaS company looking for a hands-on Operations Lead to own and
scale our internal operations as we grow from 60 to 150 people. You'll partner
closely with Finance, Sales, and Customer Success to keep the business running
smoothly and efficiently.

What you'll do:
- Own and optimize core business operations and cross-functional processes
- Manage operational budgets and drive cost efficiency
- Lead vendor selection and contract negotiation for key tools and services
- Build dashboards and KPIs that give leadership visibility into the business
- Identify and automate manual, repetitive workflows
- Hire, mentor, and grow a small operations team

What we're looking for:
- 5+ years in operations, ideally at a high-growth technology company
- Proven experience managing budgets and improving operational efficiency
- Strong cross-functional leadership and stakeholder management
- Comfort with data, reporting, and process automation tools
- Experience building or scaling a team is a plus

Nice to have:
- SQL or BI tooling experience
- Experience in a SaaS or subscription business
